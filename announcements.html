Club Registration

Registration is mandatory to participate in any Road Burners event or ride.

RBs, requesting everyone to complete club registration (if not done yet) by submitting your essential details:

  • Full name
  • Contact information
  • Emergency contact
  • Any relevant medical information

This information is crucial for ensuring everyone's safety and coordination during rides.

Opting for a welcome kit is not mandatory while registering.

Ride Safety Rules — All riders must wear full riding gear to participate in any Road Burners™ ride:

  • Full Face Helmet — ISI / ECE / DOT certified
  • Armoured Riding Jacket
  • Riding Gloves
  • Riding Pants or jeans with knee protection
  • Protective Boots

For your first ride only (day ride or breakfast ride), if you don't yet have full gear, you may still join — but helmet, jacket, gloves & boots are a must.

If a member is found violating gear rules, they may be sent back from the ride immediately.

Please be familiar with the riding discipline. 🙏🏼

No rider is left behind, and no compromise on safety. 🔥
Happy Riding 🤟🏼 — RB Crew

Club T-Shirt Distribution

Club T-shirts will be distributed only during rides, meetups, or official RB events where members are physically present.

We will not be arranging separate delivery, courier, or individual handover requests, as coordinating that for everyone becomes difficult and time-consuming.

If you haven't collected yours yet, please do so at the next ride or gathering. We'll keep announcing upcoming events regularly.

If you're attending an upcoming ride or meetup and would like to collect your T-shirt, please inform us in advance so we can bring it along.

Appreciate everyone's cooperation and understanding. 👍
